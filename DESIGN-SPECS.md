# Stride Media UI specifications

## Global

- Font: Neue Haas Grotesk Display, falling back to Familjen Grotesk.
- Page padding: 24px mobile, 48px tablet, 72px desktop, 96px wide desktop.
- Section padding: 64px mobile, 96px tablet, 128px desktop, 160px wide desktop.
- Eyebrow to headline: 15px. Headline to body: 24px. Body to CTA: 32px.
- Card gap: 24px. Major header to content gap: 48px.
- Body: 15–18px with 22–27px line-height.
- Card heading: 20–26px with 25–32px line-height.
- Section heading: 36–64px with 44–76px line-height.
- Display heading: 22–96px with 26–102px line-height.
