Privacy Policy

Last updated October 18, 2026

This policy explains how Stride Media handles personal information connected with this website, enquiries and meeting bookings. A client agreement may contain additional privacy or confidentiality terms for project work.

Who is responsible

Stride Media is responsible for personal information it collects for its own business purposes through this website and related communications. Questions and privacy requests may be sent to hello@stridemedia.co.

Information you provide

We may receive your name, email address, telephone number, company or brand details, social-media account information, meeting preferences and anything you include in an enquiry, booking, email, call or other communication. For clients, we may also process project contacts, approvals, content, billing records and information needed to provide the agreed services.

Meeting-booking information

The website includes an embedded Calendly booking interface. When you use it, Calendly may collect your name, contact details, selected date and time, timezone, answers to booking questions and technical data. Calendly processes booking data for Stride Media as its customer and may process some information for its own stated purposes. Calendly, connected calendar providers and conferencing providers may send transactional confirmations or reminders.

Information collected automatically

Our hosting and embedded-service providers may process technical information such as IP address, browser and device type, operating system, referring page, requested URL, request time, approximate location derived from IP, error and security events, and performance information. The website stores a session preference when enabled or reduced motion is selected; it ends with the browser session.

Cookies and similar technologies

Stride Media does not currently operate its own advertising cookies or behavioural-advertising tracker on this website. Calendly and third-party destinations may use cookies, local storage or similar technologies under their own notices when content loads or you interact with them. Browser settings and provider controls can be used to manage those technologies.

How we use information

  • To respond to enquiries and communicate with you.
  • To show availability, schedule and manage meetings.
  • To prepare proposals and provide requested services.
  • To operate, troubleshoot, secure and improve the website.
  • To maintain business, contractual, tax and accounting records.
  • To establish, exercise or defend legal rights and comply with applicable obligations.

Legal grounds

Depending on the circumstances and applicable law, we process information with consent, to take requested steps before a contract, to perform a contract, to comply with legal obligations, and for legitimate business interests such as responding to prospective clients, operating securely and improving services. Where consent is required, it may be withdrawn for future processing.

How we share information

We do not sell personal information. We may share it with personnel and providers that need it to perform work for us, including website hosting and infrastructure providers, Calendly and connected calendar or conferencing services, professional advisers, and business administration providers. We may also disclose information when you direct us to, when required by law or a competent authority, to protect rights and security, or as part of a business reorganisation subject to appropriate safeguards.

Hosting, media and links

The website is hosted using Vercel infrastructure. Videos displayed directly on the site are served as website assets. Links to Instagram, YouTube, TikTok, LinkedIn and other services lead to platforms that independently determine how they handle information. Review their privacy notices before submitting information.

International processing

Some providers may process information outside the United Arab Emirates, including in the United States or other countries where they or their subprocessors operate. Where applicable, we take reasonable steps to use providers and arrangements intended to protect personal information during international processing.

Retention

We retain information only as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, including to manage enquiries and client relationships, provide services, keep required business records, resolve disputes and meet legal obligations. Periods vary by record type and relationship. Information is deleted or anonymised when no longer reasonably required.

Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical and organisational measures intended to protect personal information. No internet transmission or storage system is completely secure, so absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

Your rights and choices

Subject to applicable law and relevant exceptions, you may ask whether we process your personal information and request access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, transfer or withdrawal of consent. You may also raise a concern with the relevant data-protection authority. We may need information to verify your identity.

Children

This website and our services are intended for businesses and adults. We do not knowingly request personal information from children through the website. Contact us if you believe a child has provided information so we can review it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the website, services, providers or applicable requirements change. The date above identifies the current version.

Contact

Send privacy questions or requests to hello@stridemedia.co. Please describe the request and the information or interaction concerned.
